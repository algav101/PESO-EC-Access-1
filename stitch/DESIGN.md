## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, citizen-centric GovTech design language for the **PESO EC Access Portal** of the Municipality of Dingalan, Aurora. It provides seamless public access for DOLE TUPAD emergency employment programs, 365-day lockout compliance verification, work area assignments, and community inquiry services.

### Core Principles
- **Institutional Trust & Transparency:** Deep municipal navy structures combined with vibrant civic emerald indicators instill confidence, legal validity, and governmental clarity.
- **Citizen-First Accessibility:** Built strictly to WCAG 2.1 AA and RA 10173 Data Privacy Act standards with high-contrast text, large tap targets (minimum 44px), explicit field labeling, and screen-reader optimizations.
- **Local Identity & Dignity:** Warm Tagalog headlines, municipal seal branding, and local Dingalan cultural pride (*"Serbisyong DAMA"*).

## Colors

- **Primary Municipal Navy (`#0F172A` / `#0F2942`)**: Main global masthead, section titles, authoritative button variants, and query card backgrounds.
- **Civic Emerald Green (`#059669` / `#10B981`)**: Positive confirmation states, primary CTA buttons, verified beneficiary badges, and active program metrics.
- **Civic Amber / Warning (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: 365-day lockout alerts, data privacy advisories, and anti-spam verification notices.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`)**: Clean, low-glare surface backgrounds with `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1` structural dividing borders.

## Typography

- **Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif.
- **Display Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Bold municipal proclamations with `-0.02em` letter-spacing.
- **Section Titles (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Clear structural dividers with 600-700 font weight.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** High-readability Tagalog and English text with `1.5` to `1.6` line-heights.
- **Labels & Badges (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`):** Semibold uppercase identifiers with positive tracking.

## Layout & Components

### 1. DICT GWT Utility Header
- Top utility strip (`#020617` background, `#CBD5E1` text) displaying municipality title, DPO contact email, PST GMT+8 clock, and 365-Day Lockout quick button.

### 2. Official Municipal Masthead
- White header (`#FFFFFF`) with Dingalan Seal, Philippine Republic nomenclature, Municipal Department title, PESO e-access badge, and official PESO emblem.

### 3. Hero Citizen Announcement Card
- Rounded card (`rounded-3xl`, `#FFFFFF`, border `#E2E8F0`, shadow-sm) containing the main slogan *"Pagpapalakas ng Serbisyo ng PESO..."*, highlight banner, two-column feature checklist with purple and green checkmarks, and `#PESOECAccess` pill tags.

### 4. Telemetry Metric Strip
- Navy banner (`#0F172A`) showcasing 4 live statistical counters: Verified Beneficiaries, Attendance Scans Logged, Completed Payouts, and 11/11 Barangays Covered.

### 5. Public Status Inquiry Module (`#inquiry`)
- Card with dual inquiry methods: Online Application Reference Code and Full Name + Birthday lookup with masked privacy protection (RA 10173).

### 6. Work Area Assignment Lookup (`#work-assignment`)
- High-visibility green accented container allowing beneficiaries to verify designated work barangays, team schedules, and supervisor details using full name verification.

### 7. Direct Feedback Desk (`#inquiry-form`)
- Citizen submission form with HMAC-signed arithmetic CAPTCHA challenge and Data Privacy Consent checkbox.

### 8. DICT Standard GWT Footer
- Republic of the Philippines standard government footer with Seal of the President, LGU contact information, DPO compliance contact, and Cookie/Offline storage policy disclosure.
